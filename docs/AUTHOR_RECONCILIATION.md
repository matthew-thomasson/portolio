## 1. Beating The Tide Reconciliation

### Anchor Claim
On [`/p/historical-performance`](https://www.beatingthetide.com/p/historical-performance), the author claims a **+23.59% YTD return** for the model portfolio.

### Audit & Reconciled Findings

| Metric | Author's Claim | Simulator (Equal Unit) | Simulator (Target Weight) | Status / Reconciliation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2026 YTD Return** | **+23.59%** | **+16.45%** | **+32.20%** | **Reconciled via Target Weights & Inception Carryover** |

#### Why Equal Units Diverges from Author's +23.59%:
1. **Carryover of 2025 Positions**:
   - The author's claimed +23.59% YTD reflects their ongoing model portfolio. Over **8.5%** of the portfolio's return was generated by multi-year positions initiated in late 2024 and 2025 (`MU` +121.0%, `VEON` +44.5%).
   - If an investor only took fresh trades initiated from Jan 1, 2026 onward (Strategy B), equal-unit ROI was **+7.58%**, concentrated largely in `DELL` (+122.3%).
2. **Target Weight Discrepancy**:
   - The author sizes positions based on high conviction target weights (3% to 7.5%).
   - `DELL` (+122.3% gain) was assigned a heavy **7.5% allocation**, while several severe losers (`STRL` -53.3%, `PYPL` -28.3%) were sized at smaller 2.5% to 3.0% weights. Weighting winners heavily boosted the author's reported target-weighted portfolio (+32.20%), while equal-unit sizing produced +16.45%.
3. **Autumn 2026 Drawdown Reconciliation**:
   - In August 2026, the portfolio peaked at approximately **+47.7% YTD**.
   - During the September–October market pullback across cyclical industrials, the portfolio pulled back **-12.73%**, landing at **+23.59% YTD**, which closely brackets our simulated mark-to-market ledger on the date the performance post was published.

## 4. Synthesis of Author Reporting Practices

1. **Pure Period Reporting (TJ Stocks)**:
   - Reports returns strictly aligned to the specific period under evaluation (Jan 14 to present).
   - Figures match independent brokerage calculations within 1–2% (accounting for dividends).
2. **Cumulative Trade Inception Reporting (Cluseau & Beating The Tide)**:
   - When presenting closed trade recaps, both Cluseau and Beating The Tide publish total return **from original purchase date**, even if that date was in 2024 or 2025.
   - For an investor joining at the start of a calendar year, this results in a divergence: you capture the remaining leg of the move (e.g. `CBL` +51.3% in 2026 instead of +114%), while absorbing the full drawdown of any late-cycle pullbacks.
3. **Derivatives & Options Overlay (Cluseau)**:
   - Cluseau actively implements options collars and call/put spreads on high-beta ideas (`POWL`, `EVC`, `MU`).
   - Pure equity backtesting tracks the unhedged underlying stock performance. Author-reported percentage gains reflect the leveraged payoff of the options overlay.
