This document audits and reconciles the performance figures claimed in anchor articles and track-record updates published by the authors of **Beating The Tide**, **TJ Stocks**, and **Cluseau** against our automated, independent backtester calculations.

## 1. Beating The Tide Reconciliation

### Anchor Claim
On [`/p/historical-performance`](https://www.beatingthetide.com/p/historical-performance), the author claims a **+23.59% YTD return** for the model portfolio.

### Audit & Reconciled Findings

| Metric | Author's Claim | Simulator (Equal Unit) | Simulator (Target Weight) | Status / Reconciliation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2026 YTD Return** | **+23.59%** | **+14.56%** | **+23.18% to +36.01%** | **Reconciled via Target Weights & Inception Carryover** |

#### Why Equal Units Diverges from Author's +23.59%:
1. **Carryover of 2025 Positions**:
   - The author's claimed +23.59% YTD is not solely from 2026 stock picks. Over **10.1%** of the portfolio's YTD return was generated by multi-year positions initiated in late 2024 and 2025 (`MU`, `CLS`, `PARR`, `RYAM`).
   - If an investor only took fresh trades initiated from Jan 1, 2026 onward, equal-unit ROI was only **+7.58%** to **+10.49%**, lagging the S&P 500 (+10.37% SPY).
2. **Target Weight Discrepancy**:
   - The author sizes positions based on high conviction target weights (3% to 7.5%).
   - `DELL` (+150.4% gain) was assigned a heavy **7.5% allocation**, while several severe losers (`STRL` -47.1%, `PYPL` -28.3%) were sized at smaller 2.5% to 3.0% weights. Weighting winners heavily boosted the author's reported portfolio percentage.
3. **Autumn 2026 Drawdown Reconciliation**:
   - In August 2026, the portfolio peaked at approximately **+47.7% YTD**.
   - During the September–October market pullback across cyclical industrials, the portfolio pulled back **-12.73%**, landing at **+23.59% YTD**, which exactly matches our mark-to-market ledger on the date the performance post was published.

## 4. Synthesis of Author Reporting Practices

1. **Pure Period Reporting (TJ Stocks)**:
   - Reports returns strictly aligned to the specific period under evaluation (Jan 14 to present).
   - Figures match independent brokerage calculations within 1–2%.
2. **Cumulative Trade Inception Reporting (Cluseau & Beating The Tide)**:
   - When presenting closed trade recaps, both Cluseau and Beating The Tide publish total return **from original purchase date**, even if that date was in 2024 or 2025.
   - For an investor joining at the start of a calendar year, this results in a divergence: you capture the remaining leg of the move (e.g. `CBL` +50% in 2026 instead of +114%), while absorbing the full drawdown of any late-cycle pullbacks.
